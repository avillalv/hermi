# Interview script (30 minutes)

Rules: ask about the past, not hypotheticals. Do not pitch before question 8. Do not lead. Record exact words. Never ask for a card or a payment.

## Open (2 min)
Thanks for your time. I am learning how people plan trips together. There are no right answers. I will take notes, and your name is not shared.

## Their last trip (10 min)
1. Tell me about the last trip you planned with other people. Where, who, how long?
2. Walk me through how you planned it, from first idea to departure. Which tools did you use (chat, docs, spreadsheets, TripIt, Tripsy, Wanderlog, Google Maps)?
3. Where did it get painful? What did you redo or lose?
4. How did you choose flights and where to stay? Did you watch fares? What did you do when a price changed?
5. How did the group agree on things? Who got stuck doing the work?
6. What did you pay for, if anything, to plan or manage the trip? Why?
7. After booking, where did confirmations live? How did others see the plan? Did it work offline?

## Show the idea (5 min)
8. One sentence: "Hermi is a trip planner for iOS and the web. You plan with others, track flight prices, and every fact shows the page it came from." What would you expect it to do? What worries you?
9. Which part would you try first? Which part would you ignore?
10. How much would you trust an AI suggestion that shows its source? What would make you stop trusting it?

## Switching question (3 min), if they use TripIt, Tripsy or Wanderlog
11. What do you use today, and what would make you move a trip to a new app?
12. Suppose you could bring a trip over by importing it (calendar file, pasted confirmation or a list) and got a free Trip Pass for that trip. Would that change your mind about trying Hermi? Why or why not? What would you need to trust the import?
13. What would still stop you from switching?

## Price test (7 min)
Show [price-test-cards.md](price-test-cards.md) one card at a time, in order. Record the reaction to each before showing the next.

## Close (3 min)
14. Would you use this for your next trip? When is it?
15. May I add you to the waitlist? Please send me one line in writing confirming (this is the written yes signal; see README).
16. Who else plans group trips that I should talk to?

## Record after the call
The tally row (README), top 3 pains in their words, the price pick, the switch answer, and any request that is not in Phase 1.
