## How to run 10 interviews

1. Week 1: write the yes signal below into `DECISIONS.md` before the first call. Do not change it after.
2. Recruit from the waitlist (WF-002 asks "who do you plan trips with" and "which app do you use today") and from people who plan trips with others. Mix couples and friend groups, and include at least 3 who use TripIt, Tripsy or Wanderlog.
3. Interviews 1 to 6 by week 2, 7 to 10 by week 3. About 30 minutes each, video or phone.
4. Follow the script. Show the price cards only at the end, one at a time.
5. Right after each call, fill the tally row (below). Keep notes in your own words, no names in the repo.
6. Week 3: count against the signal, then sign the go or no-go record in `DECISIONS.md`.

## The written "yes" signal

A "yes" is one interviewee who does all three, in writing (email, message or form), within 7 days of the call:

1. Names a trip they are planning, or planned in the last 12 months, with other people.
2. Says they would use Hermi for the next one, and joins the waitlist or gives an email.
3. After the price test, picks one paid option (Trip Pass $9.99, or Plus at $5.99 a month or $39.99 a year) as "I would pay this", or says in their own words what price they would pay.

Spoken enthusiasm alone is not a yes. Compliments are not a yes.

## Go or no-go rule

The spec fixes the count (10 interviews) and the timing (decision record signed in week 3; a no-go stops the backlog after week 3). It does not fix the threshold. Proposed, the owner confirms before call 1:

- **Go:** 4 or more written yes out of 10, and at least 2 of those would pay (picked a paid card, or named a price of $5.99 or more).
- **Go with change:** 2 or 3 written yes. Record what to change (positioning, price, scope) in `DECISIONS.md`.
- **No-go:** 0 or 1 written yes. Stop the backlog after week 3.

Also record how many switchers (TripIt, Tripsy, Wanderlog) said a free Trip Pass for importing a trip changed their mind. This is evidence for the import reward (07 section 10), not a gate by itself.

The owner can record a no-go at any time by stopping the driver and writing it in `DECISIONS.md`.
