# Paywall and price test cards

Prices match the root README (purchases are in the iOS app only in Phase 1). Show one card at a time. After each ask: "Would you pay this for what you just saw? Too cheap, fair, or too much?" Then the follow-ups at the bottom. No pressure, no discount, no fake urgency. The free path is always shown first.

## Card 0: Free
Free, $0. 2 active trips, 1 cached-fare route per trip, 12 credits a month, offline reading, invite 1 collaborator per trip, 1 cached-fare alert, one lifetime deep agent run (taster), and joining others' trips is free.

## Card 1: Trip Pass
**$9.99 for one trip, 90 days.** 2 live fare routes, up to 6 people planning with you, 40 AI credits for the trip. One payment, no renewal.

## Card 2: Plus, monthly
**$5.99 a month.** Unlimited trips (fair use 25), 3 live routes checked daily within 120 days of departure, 60 AI credits a month, invite collaborators. Cancel any time.

## Card 3: Plus, annual
**$39.99 a year.** Everything in Plus, with a 7-day free trial. Cancel any time in iOS settings.

## Follow-ups
- Which would you pick for your next trip, or none? Why?
- At what price would Trip Pass feel too expensive? Too cheap to trust?
- Would you still pay if only one person in the group paid?

## Record
Per card: too cheap, fair or too much. Final pick: Free, Trip Pass, Plus monthly or Plus annual. Their exact words on price.
