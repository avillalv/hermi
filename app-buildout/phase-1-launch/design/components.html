05 section 2.1 and 2.2

Color tokens

Defined in tokens.css. Each swatch shows the token in light, then dark. The hex values are read from the live tokens, so this sheet cannot drift from the file. The warm light surfaces (paper, sheet, sunken, rule) date from 2026-10-03.

Surfaces and ink

Use the semantic aliases (--background, --card, --primary) in components. Never hard-code a hex value.

  • --tp-paperlight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssPage ground (warm paper in light, deep navy in dark). Alias --background
  • --tp-sheetlight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssCards, sheets, inputs, popovers. Alias --card, --popover
  • --tp-sunkenlight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssWells, chips, hover fill, ticket stubs. Alias --secondary, --muted, --accent
  • --tp-inklight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssPrimary text, icons. Alias --foreground
  • --tp-ink-softlight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssSecondary text, captions. Alias --muted-foreground
  • --tp-rulelight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssDecorative dividers and card borders only. Alias --border, --input
  • --tp-edgelight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssBorders of interactive controls
  • --tp-brandlight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssPrimary buttons, links, focus ring, active tab. Alias --primary, --ring
  • --tp-brand-inklight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssText on brand fills. Alias --primary-foreground
  • --tp-brand-softlight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssSelected row, your own vote, soft emphasis
  • --tp-successlight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssDone, price fell, booked
  • --tp-warninglight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssCaution, price rose. Icons, bars and large text only
  • --tp-warning-inklight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssSmall warning text
  • --tp-dangerlight see tokens.cssdark see tokens.cssErrors, destructive actions. Alias --destructive

05 section 2.4

Type scale

Three families: Fredoka for names, codes and titles, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for body and labels, Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for numbers. Each role is a font shorthand token: font: var(--tp-type-title). Sentence case in the DOM; label and code are uppercased by CSS only.

  • Display--tp-type-displayFredoka 700, 40 to 56 px, line 1.0, tracking -0.01emPlan together.
  • Title--tp-type-titleFredoka 600, 30 px, line 1.1, tracking -0.005emTrips
  • Heading--tp-type-headingFredoka 600, 20 px, line 1.2Upcoming
  • Code--tp-type-codeFredoka 700, 24 to 48 px (36 to 48 px on tickets and passes), tracking 0.06em, uppercase. The kit uses 36, 40 and 44 px on tickets.RDU
  • Label--tp-type-labelAtkinson Hyperlegible Next 700, 11 px, tracking 0.08em, uppercaseNext steps
  • Body large--tp-type-body-lgAtkinson Hyperlegible Next 400, 17 px, line 1.5Build a trip with the people you are going with.
  • Body--tp-type-bodyAtkinson Hyperlegible Next 400, 15 px, line 1.55Default text for descriptions and notes.
  • Body strong--tp-type-body-strongAtkinson Hyperlegible Next 600, 15 pxArenal Hanging Bridges
  • Small--tp-type-smallAtkinson Hyperlegible Next 400, 13 px, line 1.45Aviasales, checked 3 h ago
  • Data--tp-type-dataAtkinson Hyperlegible Mono, tabular figures, 13 to 15 px25 Nov to 6 Dec 2026
  • Figure--tp-type-figureAtkinson Hyperlegible Mono 700, 32 to 48 px, tracking -0.03em$612

05 section 2.5

Spacing

4 px base. Screen gutter 16 px on phones, 24 px from 768 px. Card padding 16 px (12 px when dense). Rhythm between cards 12 px, between sections 24 px. Touch targets are at least 44 by 44 pt.

05 section 2.6

Radii

Buttons are pills. Chips and avatars are fully round. The app icon keeps its own 22% corner.

  • --radius12 px, the base
  • --radius-sm8 px
  • --radius-md10 px
  • --radius-lg12 px, inputs
  • --radius-xl16 px, cards
  • --radius-2xl20 px, bottom sheets (top corners)
  • --radius-3xl24 px
  • --radius-4xl28 px
  • 9999pxFull round: buttons, chips, avatars

05 section 2.7 and 2.8

Elevation and motion

Hermi is flat. Hierarchy comes from ground color, hairline rules and the sky band. Level 2 is for menus, toasts and floating bars; level 3 is for sheets and dialogs. In dark, level 3 trades the shadow for a lighter top edge.
