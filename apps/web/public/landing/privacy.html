Privacy policy

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist we collect your email address and, if you choose to answer, who you plan trips with and which app you use today.

We use them to send you a confirmation and one email when Hermi opens, and to learn what to build first. We do not sell them and we do not use ad or tracking tools on this page.

Who sees it

Our email provider, Resend, stores your address so it can send these emails. Our host also keeps short-lived server logs that include the entry. Nobody else receives it.

Your choices

Ask us to delete your entry at any time by replying to the confirmation email or writing to support@hermi.world, and we will remove it. We send no marketing email beyond the one launch email.

This page covers the waitlist only. The full policy for the app will replace it before launch.
